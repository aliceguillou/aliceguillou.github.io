Basic Training Skills

Learn the basics of traning with the right voice tone.

Sit

To train your dog to sit, use a calm but firm voice. Make sure you give the command while holding a treat in front of them. The tone should not be too harsh, but confident enough for the dog to respond.

Down

The "down" command requires a firm, clear tone. It’s important to be assertive but not angry. Use this tone when you want your dog to lie down calmly.

Stay

The "stay" command requires a calm and steady voice. You must be confident and firm while using the word "stay." Make sure you hold the position for a few seconds to reinforce the command.

Touch

The "touch" command is often used to get your dog's attention. You should use a happy, excited, or even playful tone to engage your dog in the activity.
